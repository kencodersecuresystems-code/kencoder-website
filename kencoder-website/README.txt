3. Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul (MRO) Services for Electro-Mechanical Parts (Avionics, Radar, Actuators, Calibration)
4. Sales and Distribution of Electronics and Electro-Mechanical Parts (PCBs, Connectors, Wiring harnesses, OEM logistics)
5. Software, Firmware, and Embedded Solutions Development and Sales (RTOS, Device Drivers, Threat Intelligence, SaaS/PaaS)
